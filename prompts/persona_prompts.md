# Persona Prompts

Each persona maps to the weights in `notebooks/recommendation.py` (`PERSONA_WEIGHTS`).

- **Student** - "I need an affordable phone with strong battery life for classes, notes and streaming."
- **Gamer** - "I want the fastest processor and plenty of RAM for lag-free gaming; price matters least."
- **Content Creator** - "I shoot photos and 4K video, so camera quality and storage come first."
- **Business Professional** - "I need all-day battery and a powerful processor for email, calls and multitasking."
- **Senior Citizen** - "I want a reliable, affordable phone with long battery life and simple everyday use."
